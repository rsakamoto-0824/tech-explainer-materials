VDL wins prizes during Supplier Day ASML

VDL TBP Electronics & VDL Rena Electronica – November 9, 2021
Onderwerp
DFX Services Electromechanical components Electronics manufacturing EMS – Electronic Manufacturing Service Industrial Electronics PCBs
  The branches of VDL ETG in Eindhoven and China and VDL TBP Electronics bv won prizes during ASML's annual Supplier Day. The so-called Supplier Recognition Award for Excellent Cost Performance has gone to VDL ETG, in recognition of the excellent commitment to successful transfer, localization and cost reduction of the RH DUV V-frame, a module for ASML's lithography machine. Geert Jakobs, CEO VDL ETG: “We have been working on various modules for ASML for years. This special year will be crowned with this award. A nice compliment to the teams at VDL ETG who worked on this.” VDL TBP Electronics from Dirksland proudly took home the Supplier Recognition Award for Excellent Collaboration, as a token of appreciation for the efforts and cooperation of colleagues and the results achieved. Joost van Haperen, director of VDL TBP Electronics: “It is of course fantastic that ASML gives us this recognition for the collaboration. A big compliment for our team. Let's keep up the good work.” We are very proud of the fantastic craftsmanship of our colleagues and would like to thank ASML for their cooperation and trust.

Narrative: Smartphone Image Sensor Market Share Q2 2025

 

  2 Min Read     September 5, 2025

 
 

Smartphone image sensor market grew over 10% in Q2 2025, led by Sony at 51% share. High-resolution products and inventory buildup drove shipments.

The smartphone image sensor market secured a growth of over 10%, enabled by increasing market ASP of CIS products in the quarter. Ther customer adoption for high-resolution products continued to grow for high-end smartphones. Moreover, the market saw increased shipments driven by inventory buildup by customers to counter uncertainty due to US Tariff policy. Sony Semiconductor secured first spot with over 51% share, followed by Samsung System LSI and OMNIVISION.
